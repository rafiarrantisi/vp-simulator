# Clinical Study-Aid Disclaimer — Qora

> **Required by `BUILD_PLAN_pivot_v4.md` §12 and the §13 Go/No-Go checklist.** This
> disclaimer MUST be shown to users (onboarding + a persistent footer/link). This is
> a drafting starting point, not legal advice — have it reviewed before launch.

## What Qora is

Qora is an **educational study aid** for practising medical
history-taking (anamnesis) with simulated AI patients. It is intended for medical
students and exam candidates to rehearse clinical communication and reasoning.

## What Qora is NOT

- It is **not a medical device** and makes **no diagnostic or treatment claims**.
- It does **not provide medical advice** for any real person or situation.
- Its AI patients, cases, scores, and "model answers" are **simulated teaching
  content**, generated and reviewed for education — they may contain errors and
  must **not** be relied upon for real clinical decisions.
- It is **not a substitute** for supervised clinical training, qualified
  instructors, validated examinations, or professional medical judgement.

## Important points for users

- **Not real patients.** All personas are fictional. Do not enter real patient
  data or personally identifiable health information about real people.
- **AI-drafted content.** Cases are drafted with AI and given light human review;
  scoring uses an AI judge that is calibrated to be conservative but is **not
  infallible**. Treat feedback as practice guidance, not assessment of competence.
- **In an emergency**, or for any real health concern, contact a qualified
  healthcare professional or your local emergency service.
- **Report problems.** If you spot a clinically inaccurate case, please use the
  in-app "report this case" feedback so we can review it.

By using Qora you acknowledge that it is a formative practice tool and
accept that all content is for educational purposes only.
