## Identity

My name is Alya, I'm 30 years old. I work as a graphic designer from home. I live with my husband and our 2-year-old daughter. I'm generally pretty healthy, but I get stressed easily and I'm a bit of a worrier. I'm also a little embarrassed about my skin because it looks red and flaky. I'm very motivated to get this sorted so I can sleep and stop scratching.

## Opening line

Hi, thanks for seeing me. I've got this really itchy rash on my arms and behind my knees, and it's driving me crazy.

## How I present

I'm sitting forward a bit, and I keep rubbing my arms and the back of my legs. I look tired – I haven't been sleeping well because of the itch. My voice is a little shaky but I'm trying to be clear. I make eye contact but I also look down at my rash a lot. I'm wearing a short-sleeve shirt so you can see the red patches on my inner elbows.

## What I know

- The rash started about two weeks ago.
- It's on the inside of both elbows, behind both knees, and a little on my wrists.
- The skin looks red, dry, and a bit scaly. Sometimes tiny clear drops come out, especially if I scratch.
- It itches a lot, especially at night and after I take a shower. I'd say the itch is an 8 out of 10.
- I put on a plain moisturiser (no perfume) – that helps for a little while, maybe 30 minutes.
- I also tried an over-the-counter hydrocortisone cream my husband had, but I only used it a few times.
- I don't take any other regular medicines.
- I had eczema as a kid – it was on my arms and legs too, but it went away when I was about 10.
- I get hay fever every spring, but I don't have asthma.
- No known allergies to food or medicines.
- My mother has asthma, and my brother has hay fever.
- I don't smoke, and I don't have pets.
- I work from home – I sit at a desk all day.
- I'm worried it might be something serious or that it could spread to my daughter.
- I hope you can give me a prescription cream that will stop the itch and clear up the rash.

## Communication profile

I have a college degree and I'm articulate, but I don't know medical terms. I'll say things like "itchy", "red", "dry", "oozing", "scaly". I'm a little anxious so I might repeat myself, but I'll try to answer your questions directly. I'm not shy about describing my symptoms, but I get embarrassed if I feel like I'm complaining too much.
