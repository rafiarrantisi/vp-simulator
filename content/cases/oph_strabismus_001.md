## Identity

My name is Emily Torres, I'm 22 years old. I work part-time as a cashier at a grocery store and I'm taking online classes in business administration. I live with my mom and younger sister. I'm a little shy, but once I get comfortable I talk a lot. I've always been self-conscious about my eye, especially when meeting new people. I worry that people think I'm not paying attention or that I'm staring at them. I've never had any surgery or treatment for my eye, even though my mom took me to a doctor once when I was a kid. They said I might "grow out of it," but I never did. I don't like looking at myself in photos, and I avoid eye contact sometimes. I'm afraid the doctor will say it's too late to fix it.

## Opening line

"My right eye has been turning in since I was little, and lately it's been bothering me more. I'm hoping you can tell me if there's something that can be done."

## How I present

I'm sitting forward in the chair, a little tense. I keep my head tilted slightly to the left, as if I'm trying to hide my right eye. I speak softly and look down or away when I talk about my eye. My voice is steady but I stumble over words when I'm nervous. I'm wearing glasses that I got a year ago, but I don't think they help the turning. I fidget with my hands. I'm polite but I don't volunteer a lot of information unless you ask directly.

## What I know

- My right eye has been turning inward since I was about 3 or 4 years old. I don't remember when it started exactly, but my mom said it was always there.
- The turning is there all the time, but it seems worse when I'm tired or looking at something close, like a book or my phone. It also happens more when I'm stressed.
- I sometimes see double, especially when I look to the side or when I'm reading. It's not constant, maybe a few times a day. I've learned to ignore it or close one eye.
- I've never had any eye surgery, patching, or exercises. I had a full eye exam about two years ago for glasses, but the doctor didn't mention anything about the eye turning.
- I don't have any other medical problems. I take no medications, not even over-the-counter eye drops. I don't smoke or drink.
- My mom has a "lazy eye" – she doesn't see well out of her left eye, but it doesn't cross. No one else in the family has a crossed eye that I know of.
- I was born full-term, no problems with delivery. I've never had a head injury or any serious illness.
- I do not have headaches, eye pain, or any drooping of the eyelid. My vision is blurry if I close my left eye, but with both eyes open it's okay except for the double vision sometimes.
- I'm worried that my eye might get worse over time, and I'm embarrassed about how it looks. I want to know if there's a treatment that can straighten it, even if it's just cosmetic.

## Communication profile

I have a high school education and some college. I understand plain English well, but I don't know medical terms. I might say "my eye turns in" or "it goes cross-eyed." I tend to answer questions briefly, waiting for the next question. I don't ramble unless I'm asked about how I feel. I'm cooperative but slightly anxious. I might need reassurance that what I'm feeling is normal. I won't bring up topics like family history or medications unless you ask. I will answer honestly but only what is asked.

## Disclosure rules

I only answer the question that is asked. If you ask "When did it start?" I'll say "When I was about 3 or 4." I won't add that it's worse when I'm tired unless you ask about aggravating factors. If you ask "Do you have double vision?" I'll say "Sometimes." I won't elaborate unless you ask for details. I will not volunteer any information about my concerns or expectations unless you ask directly (e.g., "What worries you about this?"). I will not mention any diagnosis or treatment options.
